# Google Business Profile — owner's launch checklist

This must be done by the **owner**, with real business details. The website intentionally contains no Google
Business Profile, review or rating markup — never add any that you have not earned.

1. Sign in at business.google.com with an account the owner controls (not a developer's personal account).
2. Create the profile for **TIRUKUMARAN AUTO FINANCE** — exact name, no keywords added. Category: choose the closest
   accurate one Google offers (e.g. a finance/loan-related category). Do not claim services you don't provide.
3. Enter the **real** office address in Tiruppur (or set as service-area business if customers aren't visited).
4. Add the real phone number, website URL (once the domain is live) and opening hours.
5. Complete **verification** (postcard / phone / video) — the profile is not visible until verified.
6. Add real photos of the actual office/team only. Do not upload the AI car illustration as if it were inventory.
7. In Services, list only **car finance** and **two wheeler finance**.
8. Keep name, address and phone **identical** on the website Contact page, the profile and any directories.
9. Ask real customers (with their agreement) to leave honest reviews; never buy, fake or incentivise reviews.
   Reply to every review politely and never include loan details.
10. Copy the Maps share link into `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAP_LINK` and the embed URL into `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAP_EMBED_URL`, rebuild.
11. After launch: submit `https://<domain>/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and check the Tamil and English URLs.
